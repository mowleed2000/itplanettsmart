Our Mission

At IT Planet, located at 134 Uxbridge Road, London W12 8AA, we believe that a broken screen or faulty battery shouldn't interrupt your daily life. Our mission is to provide fast, reliable, transparent, and affordable tech repair services for individuals and businesses across London.

Why Choose Us?

  • Certified Hardware Engineers: Our technicians are trained in micro-soldering, motherboard diagnostics, and screen restoration.
  • Express 30-Minute Service: Most smartphone screens and batteries are replaced while you wait in store.
  • 12-Month Warranty: Every replacement part comes backed by our 1-year peace of mind guarantee.
  • No Fix, No Fee: If we cannot repair your device, you pay nothing for the diagnostic check.
