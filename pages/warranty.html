IT Planet 12-Month Warranty

Peace of mind with every screen, battery, and hardware repair carried out at IT Planet London.

What is Covered?

Our 12-month warranty covers manufacturer defects in replaced screens, touch digitizers, batteries, and charging ports. If a replaced part fails due to a component defect within 12 months, we will replace it free of charge.

What is Not Covered?

  • Accidental damage (cracks, drops, pressure marks on screen).
  • Liquid / water damage after the repair.
  • Third-party tampering or physical modifications.
